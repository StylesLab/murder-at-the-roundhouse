# The Joker's Secret

A four-player, single-device Roundhouse game using one physical deck of playing cards and one Joker.

## Setup

1. Add one Joker to a standard deck.
2. Remove one ordinary card so every non-Joker card can form a pair.
3. Deal the full deck between four players.
4. Enter player names in the app.

## Each room

Play a round inspired by Old Maid: discard pairs, draw from the player on your left, and continue until only the Joker is unpaired.

The app then records who holds the Joker. That player receives no deduction. Every other player privately removes half of the suspects remaining on their personal 16-guest board.

The app moves the investigation through rooms in the Roundhouse and supplies the narrative.

## Winning

The first player whose suspect board contains only one guest wins.

When multiple players reach one suspect in the same room, shuffle and deal the deck again. The first tied player to receive the Joker wins the tiebreak.
